📖 RidePredict System Architecture & Machine Learning Pipeline

End-to-end overview of the data engineering, training methodology, and inference engine.

1. Problem Definition & Objectives

In on-demand transportation and cab booking ecosystems, ride cancellations lead to driver downtime, customer dissatisfaction, and revenue loss. RidePredict creates an intelligent binary classification system to accurately predict whether an upcoming booking is likely to be Completed (0) or Cancelled (1) prior to dispatch.

2. Feature Engineering & Preprocessing Pipeline

  • Time Difference (time_diff): Exact lead time between booking creation and scheduled pickup in hours.
  • Booking Nature (booking_nature): Segmented into Immediate (≤ 4h = 0), Same/Next Day (≤ 24h = 1), and Advance (> 24h = 2).
  • Area Cancellation Volume: Historical cancellation rate per pickup area categorized into Zero (0), Low (1), Medium (2), and High (3).
  • Vehicle Model Identifier (is_VMID_12): Binary flag for the most dominant vehicle category #12.
  • Temporal Features: Pickup Month, Day of Week, and Weekend indicator (is_weekend).

3. Specialized Model Training per Travel Type

Because operational behaviors differ fundamentally between ride types, 3 distinct Decision Tree models are trained:

1. Long Distance / Outstation

11 features. Low cancellation rate (~1.4%), characterized by high advance planning.

2. Point to Point (Intra-city)

12 features. Primary volume (~29.6k rides) with an 8.3% cancellation rate influenced heavily by lead time and pickup zone.

3. Hourly Rental

9 features. Governed by package duration (hours/km) and booking lead times (~4.5% cancellation).
